# Spanly — Brand & Logo Guidelines

Welcome to the official Brand Identity & Logo Kit for **Spanly** (TimeSpanSelectorView Android Library).

---

## 1. Logo Concept

The **Span Arc "S"** mark combines the initial letter **'S'** of **Spanly** with a circular time span selector ring and dual thumb control handles (start time & end time points).

- **Symbolism**: Time selection, fluid gesture control, precision snap intervals, circular + linear span versatility.
- **Form**: Geometric arcs with optical alignment and custom rounded typography.

## 4. Usage Rules

### Clear Space
Maintain a minimum clear space surrounding the logo equal to **X** (where X is the radius of the thumb handle dot, approx. 20% of the symbol height). Do not place secondary graphics, text, or borders within this margin.

### Minimum Sizes
- **Horizontal Lockup**: Minimum width 140px.
- **Standalone Symbol**: Minimum width 24px.
- **Favicon**: Micro-cut optimized down to 16px.

### Unapproved Variations (Don'ts)
- ❌ Do not rotate or distort the proportions of the symbol or wordmark.
- ❌ Do not apply drop shadows or glow effects on flat monochrome print versions.
- ❌ Do not change the color hierarchy of the start (Indigo) and end (Cyan) handles.
